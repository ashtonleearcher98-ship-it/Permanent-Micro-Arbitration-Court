# PMAC Rules of Procedure
**DRAFT — FOR DISCUSSION; NOT YET ADOPTED**

1. **Scope.** These rules govern only disputes submitted by consent to PMAC.
2. **Written request.** A requesting party identifies all parties, relevant facts, consent status, and requested relief.
3. **Consent verification.** The Registry must obtain express written consent from all necessary parties before opening arbitration. A unilateral submission is only an enquiry.
4. **Initial review.** The Registrar checks authority, jurisdiction, conflicts, scope and any legal or safety concerns.
5. **Appointment.** Parties agree to one or three arbitrators or use an independent appointing process; decision-makers must declare conflicts.
6. **Challenges.** Grounds for challenge are examined by an independent appointing authority or challenge panel.
7. **Pleadings.** Timetables must allow each party to present and respond to material evidence fairly.
8. **Hearings.** Proceedings may be conducted in writing or remotely by agreement; accessibility requirements should be considered.
9. **Evidence.** Tribunals weigh relevance and reliability; no party may be deprived of an opportunity to answer critical allegations.
10. **Confidentiality.** Filings are private by default; publication requires express approval and appropriate redaction.
11. **Decision.** Written reasons must address jurisdiction, facts and outcome; votes are decided by a majority.
12. **Review.** A limited procedural challenge may be made within fourteen days.
13. **Lawful authority.** PMAC has no criminal jurisdiction or coercive powers and cannot override recognised courts.
14. **Costs.** Fees, if introduced, must be published in advance and administered impartially.
15. **Interpretation.** The competent Assembly may revise these rules prospectively, without prejudice to pending proceedings.
