# PMAC Code of Ethics
**DRAFT — FOR DISCUSSION; NOT YET ADOPTED**

- **Independence:** no council member, donor or external official may influence a tribunal's merits decisions.
- **Impartiality:** arbitrators treat all parties equally and avoid conflicts of interest.
- **Disclosure:** relevant past or current relationships must be disclosed promptly.
- **Recusal:** decision-makers must step aside when impartiality is reasonably in doubt.
- **No ex parte communications:** substantive contact with a single party outside the agreed procedure is prohibited.
- **Confidentiality:** private evidence and information are protected against unauthorised disclosure.
- **Competence:** arbitrators accept assignments only where they have the skills and time to perform them properly.
- **Transparency:** institutional interests, funding sources and published disciplinary outcomes must be handled under clear rules.
- **Professional conduct:** participants must communicate respectfully and refrain from retaliation or intimidation.
- **Accountability:** ethics complaints should be reviewed by an independent panel with procedural safeguards.
